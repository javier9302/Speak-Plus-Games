City Run

speak
How it works
  1. The driver sits with their back to the screen and holds the keyboard.
  2. The navigator watches the screen and gives directions in English. No pointing.
  3. One key press is one step: ↑ one step forward, ← → turn on the spot, Space stop at a door.
  4. Finish the mission, then reach B. The fastest total time wins.
