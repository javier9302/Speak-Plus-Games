Board game · 2 to 6 players

Speak Plus City

Buy, trade, bid and negotiate — in English. Every action is a sentence, and the bank (you) approves it.

  1. Open the teacher window on your laptop screen. You are the bank.
  2. Open the board window and drag it to the projector or TV. Press F for full screen.
  3. Add the players, choose their levels and start.
Teacher window Board window Content editor

Both windows must be open in the same browser on the same computer. The game saves itself after every action.
