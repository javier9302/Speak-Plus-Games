# Code of conduct

This is a project by and for teachers. Treat everyone here the way you would want a colleague to
treat you in the staff room.

- Be kind and specific. Criticise the code or the activity, never the person.
- Assume good intent, and remember that many contributors write in their second or third language.
- Teachers from every country, school type and level of experience are welcome, and so are
  students and developers who are not teachers.
- No harassment, insults, or sexual, violent or discriminatory content, in issues, pull requests,
  games or content packs. Everything here may be shown to children.
- Never share personal information about students, including names, photos or recordings, in
  issues, screenshots or packs.

If something here makes you uncomfortable, open an issue marked "conduct" or contact the
maintainer through their GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove content, and block
people who break these rules.
